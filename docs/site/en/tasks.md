# Tasks and boards

Work runs on boards. A board belongs to a team, the whole company or just you, and you choose its columns.

## A task

A task has a title, a description, people (up to 10), a due date, a priority and, optionally, a customer. Add subtasks, files and comments.

The thin rail on the left of every card shows where the task stands against time: calm, soon, today, late, done. The same colours return in the calendar and in My tasks.

## When two people change the same task

Tendwell never silently overwrites anyone. If someone saved while you were editing, their change is kept and yours is applied on top. You are only asked when the same field really clashes.

## Changing many at once, and undo

Select several tasks on a board or in the list and change their status, people, date or priority together. The notice at the bottom offers **Undo** for 30 minutes.

## Repeating tasks

Make a task repeat every so many days, weeks or months. The next one opens when the current one is finished. The new date is counted either:

- **From the due date:** for fixed calendars, such as "report on the 1st of every month".
- **From the day it was finished:** for work like "call again two weeks after the last call".

## Mentions and muting

Type `@` in a comment to mention someone; they are told. If you no longer want to hear about a task, choose **Mute** in its menu.

## Quick search

Press Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) anywhere to find a customer, task, person, board or page. Move with the arrow keys, open with Enter.
