# Tasks and boards

Work runs on boards. A board belongs to a team, the whole company or just you, and you choose its columns.

## A task

A task has a title, a description, people (up to 10), a due date, a priority and, optionally, a customer. Add subtasks, files and comments.

The thin rail on the left of every card shows where the task stands against time: calm, soon, today, late, done. The same colours return in the calendar and in My tasks.

## When two people change the same task

Curahold never silently overwrites anyone. If someone saved while you were editing, their change is kept and yours is applied on top. You are only asked when the same field really clashes.

## Changing many at once, and undo

Select several tasks on a board or in the list and change their status, people, date or priority together. The notice at the bottom offers **Undo** for 30 minutes.

## Repeating tasks

Make a task repeat every so many days, weeks or months. The next one opens when the current one is finished. The new date is counted either:

- **From the due date:** for fixed calendars, such as "report on the 1st of every month".
- **From the day it was finished:** for work like "call again two weeks after the last call".

## Mentions and muting

Type `@` in a comment to mention someone; they are told. If you no longer want to hear about a task, choose **Mute** in its menu.

## Quick search

Press Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) anywhere to find a customer, task, person, board or page. Move with the arrow keys, open with Enter.

## More on a task

- **Follow:** with the button at the top of a task, hear about comments and column moves on a task that is not yours.
- **Linked tasks:** say that a task **waits for** another, **holds up** another, or is **related to** it. A waiting task says "Waiting for 1" on its card; when the task it waits for is done, its people get a "You can start" notice. Tasks cannot be made to wait for each other in a circle.
- **Start date:** **+ start** beside the due date records when the work begins; the timeline draws it as a bar.
- **Time spent:** the +15 m, +30 m, +1 h buttons, or by hand ("1.5", "45m", "1:30"). A customer's page shows the total logged for that customer.
- **Checklist:** save the steps you use often once (add subtasks → **Checklist → save as a checklist**), then add them to any task in one click.
- **History:** who changed what and when, line by line, at the bottom of the task.
- **Look-alike warning:** if the task you are about to add looks like one that is already open, quick add says so.

## A board's own fields

Board settings → **Fields and request form**: add extra fields to every task on that board (text, number, money, date, choice, link, email, person, yes/no). A field marked **Must fill to finish** has to be filled before a task can move to a "done" column; a bulk change skips such tasks and says how many. Choice fields show as coloured labels on cards and can be filtered.

## Filters and saved views

**Filter** at the top of a board narrows it by person, due date (overdue, today, 7 days…), priority and the board's choice fields. Name the filters you have on with **Views → Save**, and show the view to everyone on the board if you like.

## Request form

In board settings switch on **Take requests through a form** and choose what the form asks. Colleagues use the **Request form** button on the board; the request lands in the column you chose as a task, and whoever asked follows it automatically.

## Moving from another tool: importing tasks from a file

The up-arrow button at the top of a board (**Import tasks from a file**) takes the CSV or Excel file you exported from your old tool. The file is read in the browser; nothing is sent until you have seen which column goes where.

- Columns are recognised by themselves (Title, Description, Status, Assignee, Due date, Start date, Priority, Customer, Completed on; their Turkish names too). Change any of them; the "Becomes" column shows how the first rows will come in.
- **Status** goes to the board column with the same name. Common names such as "To do", "In progress" and "Done" are understood as well; anything else lands in the first column and is listed in the result.
- **Assignee** is matched by name or email; people who are not found are left out, and you are told who. A customer's name is matched the same way.
- Finished tasks are left out unless you tick "Bring finished tasks too".
- Nobody is notified and no rules run. You can **undo** the whole import for 30 days: tasks nobody touched are removed, the ones someone has worked on move to the archive.

Up to 5,000 rows at a time.

## Archive

Finished tasks move to the board's **Archive** by themselves once the board has kept them long enough (90 days by default; 30–365 days or "Always" in the board's settings). A board that has been in use for years still opens at once. Archived tasks keep counting in reports; search them in the **Archive** view beside Board / List and put one back with **Bring back**. A task you bring back stays on the board for the same number of days again. Tasks you archive by hand from the list view are here too.
