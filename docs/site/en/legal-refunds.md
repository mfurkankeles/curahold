# Refund policy

> **Draft — lawyer approval required.** Version 2026-11-21.draft1. Part of the Terms of service (section 5). Text in [square brackets] is to be filled in. Not legal advice.

This policy covers paid Curahold plans bought by a company. Self-serve purchases are made from our reseller and merchant of record, Paddle.com Market Ltd, which pays any refund back. Where an Order form says something different, the Order form applies.

## The trial

The trial is free and needs no card, so there is nothing to refund. A plan is chosen only to keep working after the trial.

## Monthly plans

A monthly plan can be ended at any time and stops at the end of the month already paid for. The month that has started is not refunded.

## Yearly plans

A yearly plan is paid for the whole year. Ending it stops the next renewal, and the plan stays active until the end of the year paid for. The unused part of a year that has started is not refunded, except in the cases below. We tell administrators at least [30] days before a yearly plan renews.

## When we refund

- **A core feature is removed** that the Customer pays for, the change materially reduces the Service, and the Customer ends the affected plan: the prepaid fees for the rest of the period (terms, section 2).
- **A material change to the terms is not accepted** and the Customer ends the agreement before it takes effect: the prepaid fees for the rest of the period (terms, section 19).
- **An objection to a new sub-processor** cannot be met, and the Customer ends the affected service: the prepaid fees for the rest of the period (DPA, section 5).
- **Availability is below [99.0]% in three months of any six**, and the Customer ends the agreement: the prepaid fees for the rest of the period (SLA, section 2).
- **A charge was made in error**, such as a double charge or a charge after the plan had ended: the whole amount charged in error.
- **The law requires it.** This policy does not limit any right that mandatory law gives the Customer.

## Changing plan or seats

A larger plan or more seats is billed at once, pro rata for the rest of the period. A smaller plan or fewer seats is not paid back in money: the difference is credited pro rata on the next bill. Fewer seats never bring the bill below the plan's minimum number of full seats (usually 3; more in some regions and for yearly billing), which the Billing page shows.

## Service credits

Credits under the Service level agreement are set against the next invoice and are not paid out in cash.

## How to ask

An administrator writes to [billing e-mail] with the company name and the invoice number, within [60] days of the charge. We answer within [10] business days. An approved refund is paid back by Paddle (or by us, for invoices we issued directly) to the original payment method, in the currency of the charge and with the tax charged on it; how long it takes to arrive depends on the bank or card issuer.

## Changes

We publish every version of this policy with its date. A change does not apply to a payment made before it.
