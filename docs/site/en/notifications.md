# Notifications

Tendwell writes to your chat app first and uses email as the fallback, so notifications land where the team already looks.

## When you hear from us

- A task is assigned to you
- Someone mentions you in a comment
- Someone comments on a task you work on
- An automation opens work for you
- The morning summary: today's work and anything late
- The Monday weekly summary: renewals, and customers whose score fell or who went quiet

Switch each one on or off in your settings.

## Where they go

If your company connected Slack, Microsoft Teams or Google Chat, notifications reach you there as a direct message. Otherwise, by email.

## Quiet hours

Set quiet hours, for example 19:00–08:30. Notifications in those hours wait and go out when the quiet hours end. They follow your company's time zone.

## Calendar feed

In your settings → Calendar, make a personal calendar address and add it to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. Tasks with a due date show up in your calendar. Make a new address to retire the old one.
