# Plans and billing

## The free trial

A trial starts from a request: **Try it free** on the website opens a short form (work e-mail with a code, your company, the teams and seats you want). We read every request and answer, usually within one working day. Once it is approved, your company is created with the teams you chose and its first administrator gets a sign-in link.

- **Length:** three months by default, with everything switched on and no card. The length is set when the request is approved, so a larger pilot can get longer.
- **Seats:** the trial includes the full seats agreed at approval (usually up to 10). Viewer seats are free and never counted.
- **First sign-in:** the administrator accepts the terms of service and the data processing agreement with one click; then the team can be invited.
- **Reminders:** the administrators get an e-mail 14, 7 and 1 day before the end. Settings → **Billing** shows how many days are left; in the last week a line at the top of the app says so too.

Prices are per **full seat** each month, in your region's currency where we have a local price (Indian rupees, Singapore dollars, Turkish lira) and in US dollars elsewhere, and lower when paid for a year. Tax (VAT, GST, SST, KDV) is added at checkout where it applies; Paddle collects it and passes it on. **Viewer seats are free.**

## Choosing a plan

An administrator opens Settings → **Billing**, picks monthly or yearly and presses **Choose Team** or **Choose Business**. The payment page (Paddle, our reseller) opens over the app; when it is done the plan shows on the page within seconds.

## Seats

The bill follows the people who have **joined** with a full seat: an invitation not yet accepted is not billed. Add someone and the extra seat is billed for the rest of the period; remove someone and the next bill is lower. Nobody needs to change the seat count by hand. Every plan is billed for at least a few full seats (usually 3; more in some regions and for yearly billing): Settings → **Billing** shows the number for your region, and people up to it cost nothing extra.

## Invoices, payment method, cancelling

**Invoices, payment and cancelling** on the Billing page opens Paddle's customer portal, already signed in: download invoices, change the card, cancel. A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period paid for.

Changing between Team and Business, or between monthly and yearly, is on the same page; the difference is billed (or credited) for the rest of the period.

## Limits in each plan

Each plan sets a few limits on what costs us money to run, so the price can stay low. Settings → **Billing** shows your plan's numbers and how much of each you use:

| Limit | What happens when you reach it |
| --- | --- |
| Full seats | New people can still join as viewers (free); for more full seats, choose a larger plan. |
| Connected tools (CRMs, spreadsheets, support and accounting tools) | Connecting another tool asks you to remove one or choose a larger plan. Signing in again to a tool you have is always possible. |
| How often connected tools sync | Each plan syncs on its own schedule (hourly on Business); **Sync now** is always there. |
| File space (per full seat, shared by the company) | New uploads stop until older files are deleted or you choose a larger plan. Nothing is deleted. |
| CuraAI answers a month (per full seat, shared) | CuraAI answers from the help pages instead of the model until the next month. |

During the trial everything is open; only CuraAI has a lower monthly allowance.

## Without a plan

When the trial ends without a plan, or a cancelled plan runs out, nothing is deleted. Everyone can still **read and export** everything; nothing can be changed until an administrator chooses a plan, and then work carries on where it stopped.

If a payment fails, everything keeps working while Paddle tries again, for up to 30 days from the first failed payment; the line at the top asks an administrator to update the payment method. After that the company becomes read-only, as without a plan, until a payment goes through.

## Your own server

A Curahold installed on your own server has no billing: everything is open.
