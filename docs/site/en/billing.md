# Plans and billing

## The free trial

A new company can use everything for **14 days**, with no card. Settings → **Billing** shows how many days are left; in the last week a line at the top of the app says so too.

Prices are per **full seat** each month, in US dollars, and lower when paid for a year; taxes are added at checkout. **Viewer seats are free.**

## Choosing a plan

An administrator opens Settings → **Billing**, picks monthly or yearly and presses **Choose Team** or **Choose Business**. The payment page (Paddle, our reseller) opens over the app; when it is done the plan shows on the page within seconds.

## Seats

The bill follows the people who have **joined** with a full seat: an invitation not yet accepted is not billed. Add someone and the extra seat is billed for the rest of the period; remove someone and the next bill is lower. Nobody needs to change the seat count by hand.

## Invoices, payment method, cancelling

**Invoices, payment and cancelling** on the Billing page opens Paddle's customer portal, already signed in: download invoices, change the card, cancel. A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period paid for.

Changing between Team and Business, or between monthly and yearly, is on the same page; the difference is billed (or credited) for the rest of the period.

## Without a plan

When the trial ends without a plan, or a cancelled plan runs out, nothing is deleted. Everyone can still **read and export** everything; nothing can be changed until an administrator chooses a plan, and then work carries on where it stopped.

If a payment fails, everything keeps working while Paddle tries again; the line at the top asks an administrator to update the payment method.

## Your own server

A Curahold installed on your own server has no billing: everything is open.
