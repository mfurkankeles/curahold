# Acceptable use policy

> **Draft — lawyer approval required.** Version 2026-11-19.draft2. Part of the Terms of service.

The Customer and its users may not use Curahold to:

1. break the law or anyone's rights, or store or send unlawful, infringing, defamatory or harassing content;
2. send spam or marketing messages without the recipients' permission;
3. upload malware, or probe, scan, load-test or attack the Service, or try to reach another company's data or an account that is not theirs (security research only under the responsible-disclosure page);
4. share a seat between people, get round the device limit, seat counts or other limits, or resell or sublicense the Service without a written agreement;
5. copy or reproduce the Service, its design or its content, or reverse-engineer, decompile or disassemble it (except where mandatory law allows);
6. scrape, crawl or otherwise access the Service, its pages or the documentation by automated means without our written permission — the published API with the Customer's own key, within its limits, is allowed;
7. use AI agents, bots or browser automation to explore, record or extract the Service's features, screens, workflows, content or design;
8. build or improve a competing product, or benchmark or test the Service for that purpose;
9. carry out text and data mining on the Service, the documentation or our websites (rights reserved — terms, section 9);
10. store **special categories of personal data** (health, biometrics, religion, criminal records and the like), payment-card numbers or **children's data**, unless agreed in writing;
11. use the Service from, or for people in, a country under comprehensive sanctions, or for anyone on a sanctions list;
12. monitor employees covertly, or use activity features in a way the law forbids — the Customer informs its staff beforehand and limits per-person views to the roles that need them;
13. use the AI features to produce unlawful content, to decide about a person with legal effect without a human decision, or in a way that breaks the model provider's usage rules.

**Fair use.** The API, automations, imports, exports and AI features have limits that keep the Service fast for everyone (documented in the API reference and shown in the product). Traffic far above normal use may be slowed or asked to move to a suitable plan. Requests over a limit get a "too many requests" answer with the time to wait; known crawlers and automated tools may be refused outright.

**What happens.** We first warn and ask for a fix where we can. We may remove content, slow or **suspend** access, as narrowly as possible, when a breach is serious or continues, and **end** the agreement for a serious or repeated breach (terms, section 13). Report misuse to [abuse e-mail].
