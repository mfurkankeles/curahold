# Getting started

Tendwell is where customer success and account teams run their customers, stakeholders and day-to-day work in one place. This page covers your first fifteen minutes.

## Your company address

Every company has its own address: `yourcompany.tendwell.com`. Everyone on your team signs in there. No other company's data ever appears at that address; the separation is enforced inside the database itself.

## Signing in

- **Email link:** type your email address and click the link we send. No passwords.
- **Google or Microsoft:** one click, if your company switched it on. On a device we have not seen before, we also ask for the 6-digit code sent to your email.
- An address gets at most 5 sign-in links in 15 minutes.

## First setup

Whoever creates the company picks a template: customer success, account management, sales, support or blank. The template brings customer fields (renewal date, annual value, health…), boards and ready-made automations. All of it can be changed later.

Then invite your team. If you add your company's email domain (`@yourcompany.com`), people from that domain can ask to join, or join straight away.

## Device limit

A person can stay signed in on two devices at once; an admin can set this between 1 and 5. Signing in on one more device signs the oldest one out. That keeps a seat from being shared by several people.

> **Tip:** Your settings → Devices lists where you are signed in, and lets you sign out of any of them.
