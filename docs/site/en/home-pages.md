# Home pages and cards

Home is there so people see their work the moment they open Tendwell. Each page is made of cards, and each role starts on its own home page.

## Cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| My open tasks | Your unfinished tasks, late ones first |
| Renewals coming up | Customers renewing within the days you choose, and their total value |
| Need attention | Customers whose health is at risk or worth watching |
| Gone quiet | Customers with no activity for a while |
| Stakeholder risks | Blockers and skeptics, and customers with no champion |
| Number | A single value, such as the number of customers or total value |
| Breakdown | Customers split by a choice field |
| Team load | Customers, open, late and at-risk work per person |
| Connection health | How CRM and spreadsheet syncs are doing |
| Note | A short announcement or link for the team |

Most cards work for "mine", "my team's" or "everyone's". A card is worked out within what the viewer may see: two people opening the same page see different numbers if their access differs.

## Editing a page

Admins use **Edit page** to add, order and size cards, and to choose which roles start on that page. A page can also be shown to some roles only.

> **Tip:** To check what a role sees, use **See it as them** in Settings → Roles. It opens that person's screens, read only.
