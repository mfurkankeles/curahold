# Cookies

> **Draft.** To be reviewed before it takes effect.

Curahold sets only strictly necessary cookies, so it asks for no consent:

| Name | What it does | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tw_session` | Keeps you signed in, on this device | Until you sign out, or [30] days |
| Language | Remembers the language you chose | [1 year] |

Browser storage (not cookies) keeps small conveniences on your device: the last board used, a chosen filter, a dismissed hint. Clearing your browser's data removes them.

There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. If that ever changes, we will ask first.

The payment page is provided by Paddle, our reseller, and sets its own cookies to process the payment safely; see Paddle's own policies.
