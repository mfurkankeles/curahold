# Data processing agreement

> **Draft.** To be reviewed by a lawyer before it takes effect. Text in [square brackets] is to be filled in.

This agreement is part of the **Terms of service** between the Customer (the controller) and **[Company legal name]** ("Curahold", the processor). It applies to personal data Curahold processes on the Customer's behalf, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR where relevant, and Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK).

## 1. What is processed

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Providing the Service under the Terms: storing, showing, organising and sending the Customer's data as its Users direct |
| **Duration** | As long as the Customer uses the Service, then until deletion under section 8 |
| **Data subjects** | The Customer's Users; the people at the Customer's own customers (contacts, stakeholders, portal users); anyone else the Customer chooses to record |
| **Kinds of data** | Names, job titles, work contact details, notes, meeting and call records, tasks and comments, files, and any other data the Customer puts in. The Service is not meant for special categories of data (health, beliefs and the like); the Customer will not put them in unless it has a lawful basis |

## 2. Instructions

Curahold processes the data only on the Customer's documented instructions — these terms and the Customer's use and settings of the Service — unless the law requires otherwise, in which case Curahold tells the Customer first where the law allows.

## 3. Confidentiality

Everyone at Curahold who can access the data is bound to keep it confidential. Curahold staff open a Customer's account only when an administrator grants access, for the time and level chosen, and every visit is logged.

## 4. Security

Curahold keeps appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

- every row of data tied to its company and separated at the database (row-level security), so one company can never read another's;
- encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest by the hosting providers; connection credentials additionally sealed with AES-GCM;
- passwordless sign-in, sessions bound to a device, a one-time code for new devices, and a limit on devices per seat;
- roles and per-field access chosen by the Customer, and an activity log of important changes;
- backups, and a tested way to restore them;
- tools for the Customer to export or erase a person's data.

## 5. Sub-processors

The Customer authorises the sub-processors on the **Sub-processors** page. Curahold gives at least [30] days' notice of a new one there and by email to administrators; the Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds, and if no solution is found may end the affected part of the Service with a refund of fees paid for the time after the change. Curahold binds each sub-processor to protections no weaker than these and stays responsible for them.

## 6. Helping the Customer

Curahold helps the Customer, through the Service's tools and on request, to answer data subjects' requests, and with security, impact assessments and consultations with authorities where the processing by Curahold is concerned.

## 7. Personal data breaches

Curahold tells the Customer without undue delay, and within [48] hours, after becoming aware of a breach affecting the Customer's data, with what is known, and keeps the Customer informed while it is handled.

## 8. Return and deletion

The Customer can export its data at any time. After the account ends, Curahold keeps the data for [30] days for export, then deletes it; backups are overwritten within [35] days. Curahold keeps nothing longer unless the law requires it.

## 9. Audits

Curahold makes available the information needed to show it meets this agreement — security documentation, answers to reasonable questionnaires and, where available, independent reports. Where these are not enough, the Customer may audit once a year with [30] days' notice, at its own cost, without seeing other customers' data.

## 10. Transfers

Where data goes to a country without an adequacy decision, the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (module two, controller to processor; module three for sub-processors) apply, and, for transfers from Türkiye, the standard contracts under Article 9 of the KVKK, notified to the Authority as required.

## 11. Order of precedence

If this agreement and the Terms disagree on personal data, this agreement prevails.
