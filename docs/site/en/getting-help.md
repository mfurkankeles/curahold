# Getting help

Use **Help** in the left menu to write to Curahold support. The form asks for what support needs to find the problem without coming back to you with questions. Nothing is sent that you haven't seen; you choose what goes.

## Sending a request

1. **Topic**: Something isn't working, How do I…, Data or a connection, An idea, or Billing and plan.
2. **Impact**: for errors and data problems, say whether it stops your work. Support's order follows it.
3. **Title**: a few words. While you type, docs pages that may already answer it are suggested.
4. **What happened**: what you were trying to do, what you expected and what happened instead. If there was an error message, copy it word for word.
5. **How can we see it happen**: for errors, step by step (optional, but it helps a lot).

You get a **reference** (e.g. TW-000123) and a copy by email; the answer comes to the same address. Your requests and their status are under **Help → My requests**.

## Screenshots and files

- **Take a screenshot**: the browser asks which tab to share; pick the Curahold tab. The help window steps aside, so only the page is captured.
- **Paste or drop**: paste a copied picture into the form, or drop files on it.
- **Mark up**: highlight what matters with a red frame. **Hide** covers anything support shouldn't see (a customer's name, an amount); it is removed from the picture that is sent.

Up to 6 files, 10 MB each. Pictures, PDF, text, CSV, JSON, Excel and short videos.

## Technical details added to the request

The form adds these on its own and shows them to you before sending:

| Detail | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| The page address | Where the problem is. Secret parts of the address (sign-in codes and the like) are removed. |
| Browser, screen, app version | For problems that happen in one browser only. |
| Last pages you opened | How you got there. Addresses only, no page content. |
| Errors on the page | Error messages from the background. Email addresses and key-like text are masked. |
| Requests that failed | Which action failed (path and status code). |
| What is open now | The customer, board or task you have open. |

Untick anything you'd rather not send. Nothing you typed and no data on the page is part of these details.

## Letting support look at your account

Some problems are hard to fix without seeing them. The **Let Curahold support look at my account** box at the end of the form is for that. It is never ticked for you.

- **What support sees**: exactly what you see — the same customers, boards and fields. Nothing more.
- **For how long**: 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days. Access ends on its own. Each support visit lasts at most 8 hours.
- **What support can do**: **look only**, by default. Only administrators can also allow **changes**.
- **Never within reach**: your devices and sessions, API keys, apps and webhooks, inviting people, roles, company settings, exporting or erasing data. Support cannot extend its own access either.
- **Closing it**: any time, under **Help → My requests → Close access**. A support visit in progress ends at once. While access is open, a strip at the top of the page reminds you.
- **Record**: every support visit, and every change made with permission, is written to **Settings → Activity log** with the support person's name.

While support is inside, a strip at the top of the screen shows who is looking and until when.

## Company setting (administrators)

In **Settings → Security → Support access**, choose who may let support in:

- **Anyone**: for what they can see themselves (the default).
- **Administrators only**: everyone else's open access ends now.
- **Nobody**: support can still answer by email, but cannot come in.

The same page lists the access that is open now, who gave it and the last visit, and lets you close any of it.
