# Reporting a security problem

If you think you have found a security problem in Curahold, please tell us first and give us time to fix it before telling anyone else. We are grateful for every careful report.

## How to report

Write to the address in [/.well-known/security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt) (the **Contact** line). Include what you found, the address or screen where it happens, the steps to see it again, and what someone could do with it. Screenshots or a short video help. Write in English or Turkish.

Please do not put the details in a public place, a help request or a social network.

## What we promise

- We confirm we received your report within three working days.
- We tell you what we think of it, and our plan, within ten working days.
- We keep you informed until it is fixed, and we tell you when it is.
- If you wish, we name you when we describe the fix.
- We will not take legal action against research done in good faith and within these rules.

## Rules for testing

- Use only accounts you made yourself, or a company you set up for testing. Never read, change or delete another company's or person's data; if you reach some by accident, stop, keep nothing and tell us.
- Do not slow the service down for others: no load or denial-of-service tests, no automated scanning faster than a person would click.
- No social engineering of our staff or customers, and no physical tests.
- Self-hosted installations belong to the company running them; report problems in the code to us, problems in their setup to them.

## Out of scope

Reports about missing best-practice headers without a working attack, the email-link sign-in page saying the same thing for every address (on purpose), rate limits that can be reached by hand, and problems that need a device already in an attacker's hands.
