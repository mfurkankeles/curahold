# Pipeline, reports and team

The menu on the left has four parts: everyday work at the top (Home, My tasks, Inbox, Calendar), **Customers**, **Work** (boards and the timeline) and **Insight** (Reports, Team). Click a part's heading to fold it away.

## Opportunities: the pipeline

**Customers → Opportunities** shows deals in columns by stage. Drag a card to another stage; drop it on **Won** or **Lost** to close it. The strip on top adds the money up: open pipeline, a weighted forecast (later stages count for more), what closes this month and what was won.

Every deal has a **type**: new business, expansion or renewal. Change the stages and types in Settings → Record types; with a CRM connected the stages come from there. **Table** shows the same deals as a list. Any kind of record with a choice field can be shown as a board the same way.

## Targets and pace

In Settings → **Targets and rhythm**, choose the period (month, quarter or year) and what you aim to win for each kind of deal: new business, expansion, renewal. Leave a kind empty to have no target for it.

The **Target** section at the top of Reports shows three things on one bar for each kind: what is won (solid), the weighted total of open deals due to close in the period (hatched), and where an even pace would be today (the vertical mark). The label beside it says how it is going:

- **Ahead:** more is won than an even pace needs by today.
- **On track:** less is won than that, but open deals cover the rest.
- **Behind:** neither. Underneath: how much is missing and what has to be won each week that is left.

On the right, who won how much in the period. The same bar sits as one line above the Opportunities board. Nothing is stored; the numbers are worked out from the deals when the page opens.

## Inbox

Everything sent to you in one list: tasks given to you, mentions, comments, moves on tasks you follow, reminders from rules. Unread ones are marked on the left; mark one read or unread, or clear them all in one click.

## Timeline

**Work → Timeline** shows dated tasks in a six-week window. A task with a start date is a bar, one with only a due date is a marker on that day; the colours are the time colours used everywhere. Group by board, person or customer.

## Reports

**Insight → Reports** works out where things stand right now: open and overdue tasks, finished in the last 30 days, the share finished on time and the average days from opened to done; what came in and got done week by week; who is carrying what; the split by board. For customers: health, the next six months of renewals and the pipeline by stage. Pick a board and the numbers narrow to it. Everyone sees the numbers of the boards and customers they can see, and no others.

## Team and 1:1s

**Insight → Team** shows open, overdue, upcoming and finished work per person; the bar of someone carrying clearly more than average turns amber. Click a person for a 1:1 view: what is overdue, what is coming up, what has not been touched for two weeks, what was finished in the last 14 days, how many customers they look after and their latest contact.

## Build your own report

**Reports → Reports → New report.** Five steps, and the result on the right is redrawn as you change them:

1. **What are you looking at?** Tasks, customers, opportunities (and the company's other record types), customer contact, or time logged.
2. **Narrow it down.** Add conditions: “Status is one of: Open”, “Close date in this quarter”, “Annual value is at least 50,000”, “Assignee: Me”. Dates offer “in the last 30 days”, “in the next 90 days”, and “this / the last full week, month, quarter, year”.
3. **Group it.** Group by a field (a date by day, week, month, quarter or year); split each group by a second field if you like.
4. **What to show for each.** The number of rows, or the sum, average, smallest or largest of a number field. Choose how many groups to draw and their order here; the rest become “Other”.
5. **How to draw it.** Bars, columns, stacked, a line, a ring, one figure, or a table.

Without a grouping the report is a list of rows, and you pick its columns. **Export** downloads the result as CSV.

**Save report** keeps it as yours. Tick **Share with the company** and everyone can open it. In a shared report each person sees only the tasks and customers they can see anyway; a field their role may not see does not show up in a report either.

**Ready-made reports** are further down the same page: open one, see it with your own data, change it and keep it as your own.

## Dashboards

A dashboard is a page made of charts and lists (the tabs on your home). Under **Reports → Dashboards**:

- **Ready-made dashboards arrive filled:** Sales pipeline, Renewals and risk, Customer health, Team performance, Customer contact, Work flow and Executive summary are there the first time anyone opens Dashboards, drawn from your own data. Nothing has to be added first.
- **Dashboards opens on a dashboard:** the one you looked at last. The names along the top move from one to another in one click.
- **Change or delete any of them:** **Edit dashboard** changes its cards; **Delete** sends it to the trash, where it can be taken back for 30 days. A ready-made one you deleted does not come back by itself: **Add a dashboard** lists the ones you no longer have.
- **Empty dashboard:** under **Add a dashboard**; you pick the cards. The “Report” card draws a report you kept or a ready-made one, and can draw it differently on that card.
- With a report open, **Add to a dashboard** puts it straight onto one (the report is shared with the company, so everyone who opens the dashboard sees the card).

People who run the company settings add and edit dashboards; anyone who can write can build a report.
