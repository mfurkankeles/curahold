# Connecting Claude and ChatGPT

Connect Curahold to Claude, ChatGPT or any assistant that speaks MCP, and the assistant can look things up and get work done in Curahold as you.

## What you can ask

- "Which of my customers renew in the next 60 days and look risky?"
- "Summarise Harborline Bank's stakeholders and open work."
- "Make a 5-slide summary of my portfolio."
- "Open a task for Zeynep, due tomorrow, to look into Velto Mobility's usage drop."
- "Mark this task done and comment 'Proposal sent'."

## How to connect

1. In Curahold, open **Your settings** and copy the **Connector address**. It looks like `https://yourcompany.curahold.com/mcp`.
2. **Claude:** Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Paste the address and connect.
   **ChatGPT:** add a custom (MCP) connector with that address in the connectors settings. Some plans need developer mode switched on first.
3. Curahold's permission screen opens. Sign in, check what the assistant may do and choose **Allow**.

## Safety

- The assistant works **as you**. It sees only what you can see and changes only what you can change. Your role and field rules apply unchanged.
- Untick "Also allow changes" on the permission screen and the assistant only reads. On a viewer seat it only reads anyway.
- Disconnect it any time under Your settings → API keys and connected apps.
- The assistant runs on your own Claude or ChatGPT plan; Curahold charges nothing extra for AI.
