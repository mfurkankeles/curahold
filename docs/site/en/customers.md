# Customers, fields and records

Customers are at the centre of Curahold. Each has an owner, an optional team and fields of your own.

## Fields

Add fields in Settings → Customer fields: text, number, money, date, choice, email, link. Choice options can have a colour, so a field like health shows up in that colour in lists and cards.

You can rename "Customer" too: "Account", "Brand", "Partner", whatever your team says appears everywhere in the interface.

## Records: contacts, opportunities and more

Keep records under customers:

- **Contacts (stakeholders):** job title, decision role (decision maker, budget approver, influencer, evaluator, user) and stance towards you (champion, supporter, neutral, skeptic, blocker).
- **Opportunities:** amount, close date, stage and status.
- **Your own kinds of record:** "Projects" or "Contracts", say.

Records can feed customer fields: "Open pipeline", "Champions", "Blockers". The field updates by itself when a record changes.

## Views: cards, table, renewals, products

Switch between four views with the buttons at the top of the Customers page; your choice is remembered on that device.

- **Cards:** one card per customer, saying what needs a look: late tasks, health at risk or to watch, a renewal coming up or past, tasks untouched for 14 days, no contact for 30 days. The most serious first; "All clear" when there is nothing. Cards come "needing attention first" by default and can be sorted by name, health, value, renewal or open tasks. Choose which fields show on a card from the **View** menu.
- **Table:** a sortable list where you can change many customers at once.
- **Renewals:** the next 12 months of renewals, month by month, with each month's total and how much of it is at risk or worth watching.
- **Products:** who has which product, by a choice field of a record type (for example "Subscription → Product"). The **+** in an empty cell opens a cross-sell task for that customer.

The sentence at the top says how many customers need attention and how much value is at risk; the "need attention" link narrows the list to them.

## Importing

Drop in an Excel or CSV file, match columns to fields, check the preview, import. An import that went wrong can be undone in one click for 30 days.

## Exporting

Download customer and record lists, with the filter you have on, as Excel or CSV. The file is made in your browser, and fields you cannot see are not in it either.

## Changing many at once

Select several customers in the list and change their owner, team or a field together. The change can be undone for 30 minutes.

## Plans

A **plan** is a set of dated tasks put on a customer in one click: onboarding, renewal prep, a QBR. An admin writes it once (task title, how many days after the start, for whom); on a customer's page **Apply a plan** lets you pick the start day and add them all. `{account_name}` in a title becomes the customer's name.

## Business reviews

Decide once how often you sit down with every customer: Settings → **Targets and rhythm** → *Business reviews* (every 90 days, say). One customer can have its own rhythm in the **Business review** field on its page; "No reviews" keeps that customer out of the rhythm.

**Customers → Reviews** lists the ones that are due: late, in the next 30 days, and later. When you have held the review, log it with **Done** on the row (or from the customer's page) and a short note; it lands on the timeline as a "Business review" and the next date moves by itself. For a customer never reviewed, the count starts on the day it was added. A late review also shows as a mark on the customer's card.
