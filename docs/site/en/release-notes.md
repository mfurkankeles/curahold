# Release notes

## November 2026

### Playbooks, renewals that start by themselves, currencies, and reports apart from dashboards

- **New task asks for the details:** "Add task" under a column and "New task" open the same dialog: description, customer, the record it belongs to, assignees, due date, priority, repeat, board, column and the board's own fields. Writing "friday @Ayşe #Acme !high" in the title still fills the fields for you.
- **Playbooks:** write your team's way of doing a thing as steps with dates — counted from the day it is put on, or back from the customer's renewal date or a deal's close date. Start from four ready-made ones (renewal, onboarding, business review, closing a deal) under Settings → Playbooks.
- **Renewals that start by themselves:** choose how far ahead (180 days, say) and which playbook; each morning the renewal work opens for the customer's owner, with a renewal opportunity if you like. Steps whose dates have already passed become one "catch-up" task instead of a pile due today.
- **Currencies:** a customer or a deal can be in its own currency; totals, targets and reports are in the company's, at the rates you keep under Settings → Currencies.
- **An opportunity on its own:** opens over any page with its fields, its tasks (add one, or a whole playbook) and its own thread — @mentions, links shown as small cards, files and pasted screenshots.
- **Renewals view:** beside each renewal, its renewal opportunity (opens on a click) and its agreement.
- **Targets your way:** no targets, one for all deals, or one per type of deal with each type on or off. Business reviews can be switched off for companies that don't hold them.
- **Reports and dashboards apart:** a report is the data — a list, grouped rows with subtotals, or a grid — with a chart if you want one; a dashboard puts several reports' charts side by side and can show them for one person or one team. Gauges against a goal.
- **Favorites:** star a customer, a record, a board, a report or a dashboard; it stays at the top of your menu.
- **Ready-made kinds of record:** agreements, risks, product requests and quotes in one click.
- **Smaller things:** the Done button in Reviews no longer slips off the edge; "+" on the products view asks before opening a task and offers to open it; contacts show who added them, and you choose the columns of every list; opportunities can be narrowed to a week, a month, a quarter or any dates.


### Your own reports, ready-made dashboards, and a new look

- **Report builder:** pick what to look at, narrow it, group it, sum it up and choose the chart; save it, share it, take it as CSV. 26 ready-made reports to start from.
- **Dashboards:** seven ready-made ones (sales pipeline, renewals and risk, customer health, team performance…) are added in one click; put your own reports on them as cards.
- **New colours and motion:** indigo and violet-blue; pages, figures and charts now arrive with motion (it stays still if your device asks for reduced motion).
- Amounts are shown with their currency symbol.

### Importing tasks, an archive, and speed

- **Import tasks from a file:** move in from another tool with a CSV or Excel export; columns are recognised by themselves, nobody is notified, and the import can be undone for 30 days.
- **Archive:** finished tasks move to the board's archive after 90 days (change it in the board's settings); they still count in reports and come back in one click.
- **Speed:** pages are markedly faster in large companies. Measured with 300 people, 6,000 customers and 120,000 tasks: for a board, my tasks, customers, search and the inbox the server answers in about a tenth of a second or less.

### Install it, read offline, live refresh

- **Install:** Curahold installs as an app on phones and computers (Android/Chrome/Edge: click your name → Install Curahold; iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen).
- **Offline reading:** without a connection the app opens and shows what you last saw; signing out removes the copy on the device.
- **Live refresh:** a card someone else adds, a task they move, a comment they write and new notices show up by themselves within half a minute.
- When the connection drops, error messages now say so.

### Targets, a review rhythm and nudges

- **Targets:** a target per period (month, quarter, year) and kind of deal; in Reports and above the pipeline, what is won, the weighted forecast and an even pace share one bar. "Ahead / On track / Behind", and what each remaining week has to bring.
- **Business reviews:** a rhythm for the whole company or one customer (every 90 days, say); Customers → Reviews lists the ones that are due, and logging one moves the next date.
- **Nudges:** on Thursdays, the list of your tasks that have gone still; if you want, work several days late is passed once to the team lead.
- **Boards:** a finished column shows its newest cards; the older ones are one click away.

### CRM, reports and more on tasks

- **Pipeline:** deals in columns by stage; drag and drop, Won/Lost, a weighted forecast. Deal types: new business, expansion, renewal.
- **Reports:** open, overdue and finished work, the share on time, the weekly flow, load by person and board, health, renewals, pipeline by stage.
- **Team and 1:1s:** load per person and a one-person view to prepare from.
- **Timeline (Gantt):** bars from start to due date, by board, person or customer.
- **Inbox:** every notice on one page, with filters.
- **Tasks:** a board's own fields and must-fill fields, following, "waits for" links, start dates, time spent, saved checklists, task history, a look-alike warning.
- **Boards:** filters, saved and shared views, a request form.
- **Customers:** plans applied in one click (onboarding, renewal prep), total time per customer.
- **Menu and type:** a grouped menu on the left; new typefaces (Figtree and Bricolage Grotesque, open licence).

### Easier every day

- **Quick add:** write a task the way you would say it; "friday", "next week", "nov 15", @person, #customer, !priority and "every monday" are read. Several lines make several tasks. Press N, use + New, or the + at the bottom on a phone.
- **Done in one click:** a round tick on lists and cards, with undo. **Mark done** in a task's window.
- **A new frame:** a menu with icons, one **+ New** button, late and due-today counts beside My tasks, a bottom bar on phones.
- **Dark mode** and **keyboard shortcuts** (press ? to see them all).
- A **Getting started** card and a **What's new** window.
- **Calendar:** the + on a day adds a task on that day.
- Empty screens say what to do next; while a page loads, its shape shows.

### Customers

- **Card view:** late tasks, health, renewal, stalled tasks and silence on every customer; the ones needing attention first.
- **Renewals view:** 12 months, month by month, with the value at risk.
- **Product map:** who has which product, and a cross-sell task from any gap in one click.

### Customer portal

- **Opening boards to customers:** the column new requests land in, the column names and details customers see, per board.
- **The customer's side:** their own domain, passwordless sign-in links; their company's shared requests, new requests and replies.
- **The team's side:** share a task with its customer, choose **Internal note / Reply to customer**, notifications for what customers write.
- **Security:** a separate domain and session, a database role with no table privileges, isolation per customer, a company-wide switch.
- **Ways to sign in, the company's choice:** email code and link, Google, Microsoft, passkeys and passwords; how long a sign-in lasts, and a new sign-in notice.

### Help and support access

- **Help form:** topic, impact, title, description and steps to repeat; matching docs suggested while you type.
- **Screenshots:** capture the tab, paste or drop; highlight, and cover what should stay private.
- **Technical details:** browser, last pages, errors and failed requests; shown before sending, any of it removable.
- **Support access with consent:** only when the box is ticked, time-limited (24 hours to 7 days), read only by default, closed at any moment, every visit logged.
- **Company setting:** who may let support in (anyone, administrators only, nobody).

### Support tickets

- **Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom and Jira Service Management** connections: with an address and an API key, read only.
- **Salesforce and Dynamics cases** as support tickets.
- **Any tool** through the API or an inbound webhook address.
- Tickets are filed under the customer by name, web address or the requester's email domain, and show on the timeline, in open-ticket counts and in the health score (Support load).

### Timeline, health score and weekly summary

- **Customer timeline:** log notes, calls, meetings and emails; tasks, comments, new contacts and score changes in the same stream. Pin what matters.
- **A health score that shows its reasons:** 0–100 for every customer, weights chosen by the company, the reason for every point one click away, a chart of recent weeks. Can be written to your CRM.
- **Monday weekly summary:** renewals, falling scores with the main reason, customers gone quiet.
- **AI connector:** assistants now read the timeline and the score, and can log a call note.

### Developer platform and AI connector

- **Claude and ChatGPT connector (MCP):** ask your assistant about your customers, have it prepare summaries and presentations, and open tasks. It works as you, within your rights.
- **Personal API keys:** read or write, with or without an end date; in Your settings.
- **Webhooks:** signed messages when tasks, customers, records and comments change, with retries and test sends.
- **Docs:** this site, in English and Turkish.

### Everyday work

- Search from anywhere with Ctrl K / ⌘ K.
- Change many tasks or customers at once, and undo for 30 minutes.
- Repeating tasks, counted from the due date or from the day finished.
- Download lists as Excel or CSV.
- Quiet hours, and muting a task.

### Who sees what

- Customer access per role: all, their team's or only their own.
- Per-field rights to see and to change.
- Home pages per role, with ten kinds of card.
- "See it as them": view someone's screens, read only.
