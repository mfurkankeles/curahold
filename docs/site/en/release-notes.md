# Release notes

## November 2026

### Developer platform and AI connector

- **Claude and ChatGPT connector (MCP):** ask your assistant about your customers, have it prepare summaries and presentations, and open tasks. It works as you, within your rights.
- **Personal API keys:** read or write, with or without an end date; in Your settings.
- **Webhooks:** signed messages when tasks, customers, records and comments change, with retries and test sends.
- **Docs:** this site, in English and Turkish.

### Everyday work

- Search from anywhere with Ctrl K / ⌘ K.
- Change many tasks or customers at once, and undo for 30 minutes.
- Repeating tasks, counted from the due date or from the day finished.
- Download lists as Excel or CSV.
- Quiet hours, and muting a task.

### Who sees what

- Customer access per role: all, their team's or only their own.
- Per-field rights to see and to change.
- Home pages per role, with ten kinds of card.
- "See it as them": view someone's screens, read only.
