# Release notes

## November 2026

### Easier every day

- **Quick add:** write a task the way you would say it; "friday", "next week", "nov 15", @person, #customer, !priority and "every monday" are read. Several lines make several tasks. Press N, use + New, or the + at the bottom on a phone.
- **Done in one click:** a round tick on lists and cards, with undo. **Mark done** in a task's window.
- **A new frame:** a menu with icons, one **+ New** button, late and due-today counts beside My tasks, a bottom bar on phones.
- **Dark mode** and **keyboard shortcuts** (press ? to see them all).
- A **Getting started** card and a **What's new** window.
- **Calendar:** the + on a day adds a task on that day.
- Empty screens say what to do next; while a page loads, its shape shows.

### Customers

- **Card view:** late tasks, health, renewal, stalled tasks and silence on every customer; the ones needing attention first.
- **Renewals view:** 12 months, month by month, with the value at risk.
- **Product map:** who has which product, and a cross-sell task from any gap in one click.

### Customer portal

- **Opening boards to customers:** the column new requests land in, the column names and details customers see, per board.
- **The customer's side:** their own domain, passwordless sign-in links; their company's shared requests, new requests and replies.
- **The team's side:** share a task with its customer, choose **Internal note / Reply to customer**, notifications for what customers write.
- **Security:** a separate domain and session, a database role with no table privileges, isolation per customer, a company-wide switch.
- **Ways to sign in, the company's choice:** email code and link, Google, Microsoft, passkeys and passwords; how long a sign-in lasts, and a new sign-in notice.

### Help and support access

- **Help form:** topic, impact, title, description and steps to repeat; matching docs suggested while you type.
- **Screenshots:** capture the tab, paste or drop; highlight, and cover what should stay private.
- **Technical details:** browser, last pages, errors and failed requests; shown before sending, any of it removable.
- **Support access with consent:** only when the box is ticked, time-limited (24 hours to 7 days), read only by default, closed at any moment, every visit logged.
- **Company setting:** who may let support in (anyone, administrators only, nobody).

### Support tickets

- **Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom and Jira Service Management** connections: with an address and an API key, read only.
- **Salesforce and Dynamics cases** as support tickets.
- **Any tool** through the API or an inbound webhook address.
- Tickets are filed under the customer by name, web address or the requester's email domain, and show on the timeline, in open-ticket counts and in the health score (Support load).

### Timeline, health score and weekly summary

- **Customer timeline:** log notes, calls, meetings and emails; tasks, comments, new contacts and score changes in the same stream. Pin what matters.
- **A health score that shows its reasons:** 0–100 for every customer, weights chosen by the company, the reason for every point one click away, a chart of recent weeks. Can be written to your CRM.
- **Monday weekly summary:** renewals, falling scores with the main reason, customers gone quiet.
- **AI connector:** assistants now read the timeline and the score, and can log a call note.

### Developer platform and AI connector

- **Claude and ChatGPT connector (MCP):** ask your assistant about your customers, have it prepare summaries and presentations, and open tasks. It works as you, within your rights.
- **Personal API keys:** read or write, with or without an end date; in Your settings.
- **Webhooks:** signed messages when tasks, customers, records and comments change, with retries and test sends.
- **Docs:** this site, in English and Turkish.

### Everyday work

- Search from anywhere with Ctrl K / ⌘ K.
- Change many tasks or customers at once, and undo for 30 minutes.
- Repeating tasks, counted from the due date or from the day finished.
- Download lists as Excel or CSV.
- Quiet hours, and muting a task.

### Who sees what

- Customer access per role: all, their team's or only their own.
- Per-field rights to see and to change.
- Home pages per role, with ten kinds of card.
- "See it as them": view someone's screens, read only.
