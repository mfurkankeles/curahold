# Terms of service (master subscription agreement)

These terms are an agreement between **[Company legal name]**, [registered address], [registry / tax number] ("Curahold", "we") and the company or organisation that uses Curahold ("the Customer", "you"). The person who accepts them confirms, by ticking the box, that they are authorised to bind the Customer. Self-serve customers accept these terms with a click; enterprise customers sign an **Order form** that refers to them. The **Data processing agreement**, the **Service level agreement** and the **Acceptable use policy** are part of these terms.

**Order of precedence:** the Order form, then the Data processing agreement (for personal data), then these terms, then the Service level agreement, then the Acceptable use policy, then the documentation.

## 1. Definitions

"Service": the Curahold application, its customer portal, API and AI features. "Customer data": everything the Customer and its users put into the Service. "User": a person the Customer lets use the Service. "Administrator": a user with the right to manage the Customer's account. "Subscription term": the period paid for.

## 2. The service and changes to it

We provide the Service as described in the documentation and may improve it. We will not remove a core feature the Customer pays for during a subscription term without at least [30] days' notice and, if the change materially reduces the Service, the right to end the affected subscription with a refund of prepaid fees for the rest of the term.

## 3. Accounts, administrators and seats

The Customer is responsible for its users, for keeping sign-in details safe and for what is done under its account. A seat is for one named person and may not be shared (the device limit enforces this). Administrators can add and remove users, roles and connections.

## 4. Trial

- A trial is requested through the trial form and **opens after our approval**; we may decline a request without giving reasons.
- The trial is free and lasts **3 months by default** (we may agree a different period in writing), for **up to 25 seats**.
- Reminders are sent before the trial ends. When it ends, the Customer's data stays available **read-only and exportable**, but cannot be changed, until a plan is chosen or the account is closed (data is then handled under section 13).
- During a trial the Service is provided as it is; the service credits of the SLA do not apply.

## 5. Plans, fees and renewal

- Paid plans renew **monthly** unless the Order form says otherwise. The Customer may end a plan **at the end of any month** (the monthly exit right); no refunds for a part-month unless the law or the Order form requires it.
- We may change prices with at least **30 days'** notice; a change applies from the next period after the notice ends.
- **Reseller:** self-serve purchases are made from our reseller and merchant of record, Paddle.com Market Ltd, under its buyer terms; Paddle issues the invoice and collects the tax. The right to use the Service, the DPA and the SLA remain between the Customer and Curahold. Enterprise customers invoiced directly use an Order form.

## 6. Taxes

Prices are exclusive of taxes. Where the Customer must withhold tax on a payment, it pays the additional amount needed so that we (or our reseller) receive the full price, unless a tax treaty reduces the withholding and the Customer gives us the documents in time. Reverse-charge VAT is the Customer's to account for. A local e-invoice in the Customer's country is issued only where a Curahold entity or partner exists there [lawyer/accountant: confirm per country].

## 7. Customer data

- Customer data belongs to the Customer. The Customer grants us the right to host, copy, transmit and display it only to provide, secure and support the Service, and as the DPA allows.
- We process personal data in Customer data as a **processor** under the DPA. For account, billing, security-log and product-usage data we are a separate **controller**, as described in the Privacy notice.
- We may use **aggregated, non-personal** statistics about the use of the Service to run and improve it; never Customer data itself.
- The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis for the data it puts in and for informing the people concerned, including its own employees where Curahold features record their activity (and any works council where the law requires).

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential, uses it only for this agreement and shares it only with people who need it and are bound to the same duty. This lasts for 5 years after the agreement ends (indefinitely for trade secrets and for Customer data).

## 10. Connected third-party tools

When the Customer connects another tool (CRM, chat app, accounting system), data moves between them on the Customer's instruction and under the Customer's own agreement with that tool's provider. Those providers are not our sub-processors and we are not responsible for them.

## 11. AI features

- AI features can be switched on or off per company by an administrator. Only the minimum data needed for an answer is sent to the AI model provider, which is listed on the Sub-processors page with its region and retention.
- **Customer data, prompts and outputs are not used to train AI models**, by us or by our model providers; our providers are bound to this in writing.
- AI output can be wrong; a person decides. The Service does not take decisions with legal or similarly significant effect on a person by AI alone. People are told when they are talking to an AI.
- The Customer keeps its rights in its inputs and in the outputs it receives.
- Türkiye: an AI system hosted abroad is a transfer under Article 9 of the KVKK; it is covered by the safeguards in the DPA, not by consent alone.

## 12. Security, support and availability

We protect the Service and Customer data with the technical and organisational measures in Annex 2 of the DPA. Support and availability are set out in the **Service level agreement**. Curahold staff can reach a Customer's account only when an administrator grants access, for the time chosen, and every action is logged.

## 13. Term, suspension, ending, export and deletion

- The agreement runs while the Customer has a trial or a plan. Either party may end it for a material breach not fixed within **30 days** of written notice, or at once for unlawful use or insolvency where the law allows.
- We may suspend access, as narrowly as possible and with notice where practical, for a serious security risk, unlawful use or a breach of the Acceptable use policy.
- The Customer can **export** its data at any time and for **30 days** after the end. After that we delete it; backups are overwritten within **35 days**. We confirm deletion in writing on request.
- Sections 6–9, 13, 15–17 and 20 continue after the end.

## 14. Warranties

Each party has the power to enter into this agreement. We will provide the Service with reasonable skill and care and substantially as documented. Otherwise, and to the extent the law allows, the Service is provided without other warranties.

## 15. Indemnities

We defend the Customer against third-party claims that the Service infringes their intellectual property and pay the resulting damages or settlements, unless the claim arises from Customer data, a combination with something we did not provide, or use against these terms. The Customer defends us against third-party claims arising from Customer data or from use of the Service against these terms. The defended party tells the other promptly and lets it control the defence.

## 16. Liability

- Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, lost profits or lost revenue.
- Each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid or payable in the **12 months** before the event. For breaches of the data-protection and confidentiality duties the limit is **[2–3] times** that amount.
- These limits do not apply to fraud, intent, **gross negligence where the law does not allow it to be excluded** (in Türkiye, TBK art. 115), death or personal injury, the Customer's duty to pay fees, or the indemnities in section 15 [lawyer: separate cap for the IP indemnity?].
- Service credits are the sole remedy for missed availability (SLA).

## 17. Sanctions, export control and anti-corruption

The Customer will not use the Service from, or for people in, a country or region under comprehensive sanctions, nor for anyone on a sanctions list. Iran is excluded by policy. Each party complies with the anti-corruption laws that apply to it.

## 18. Publicity

We will not use the Customer's name or logo without its written consent.

## 19. Changes to these terms

- Every version stays public with its effective date and a list of changes.
- **Material changes** are announced by e-mail to administrators and in the app at least **30 days** before they take effect. From the effective date an administrator must accept the new version before making changes; reading and export stay open meanwhile. If the Customer does not accept, it may end the agreement before the effective date with a refund of prepaid fees for the rest of the term.
- Non-material changes (clarity, new features, legal requirements that do not cut the Customer's rights) are announced on the change list.
- A signed agreement (Order form) changes only by a written amendment. Sub-processor changes follow the notice-and-objection route of the DPA.
