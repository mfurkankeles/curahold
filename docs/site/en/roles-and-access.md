# Roles and access

Roles decide who can see and change what. The rules are enforced in the database, not just in the interface: anything hidden on screen is also hidden in the API, in exports and to the AI connector.

## Seats

- **Full seat:** sees and changes.
- **Viewer seat:** sees only. Good for managers and leadership.

## Built-in roles

| Role | In short |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Everything: company settings, people, roles, connections, the activity log |
| Team lead | Sees every board; manages teams, customer fields and automations |
| Member | Their own work and the customers they can reach |
| Viewer | Sees, does not change |

Rename roles, add your own and switch individual permissions on or off.

## Customer access

For each role, choose which customers it reaches:

- **All customers**
- **Their team's customers:** the customers of the teams they are in, plus their own
- **Only their own customers**

Contacts, opportunities and tasks under a customer follow the same rule.

## Per-field access

Hide a field from some roles, or let them see it without changing it. For example, members see "Annual value" but cannot change it, and only admins see "Discount".

## See it as them

In Settings → Roles, **See it as them** next to a person opens Tendwell the way they see it. Nothing can be changed meanwhile, and the strip at the top takes you back to your own view.
