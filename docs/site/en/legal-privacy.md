We do not sell personal data. We take no decision with legal or similarly significant effect about anyone by automated means alone. Curahold is a business service and is not for anyone under 18; we do not knowingly collect data about people under 18.

## The trial-request form

The form asks for your name, work e-mail, job title, company, country and a few questions about your company. Before you send it you see a short notice for your country and, where your country's law asks for it, a separate consent box (for example in the United Arab Emirates). Marketing e-mail is a separate, optional, unticked box and never a condition of the trial. We record what you were shown (the text version and a fingerprint of it), your choices, the time and your country, to be able to prove them. If no trial starts, the request is deleted after 12 months.

## Marketing e-mail

Only with your permission. Every message names us, gives our address and has a one-click unsubscribe link; we honour it at once (in Türkiye within 3 business days at the latest) and keep you on a do-not-send list. In Türkiye we do not ask for this permission, and send no marketing e-mail, until we are registered in the commercial-message system İYS; after that the permission is registered there too.

## Cookies and browser storage

The website and the product use only what they need to work, unless you agree to more. **Analytics** (Google Analytics 4) and **advertising pixels** (Meta, TikTok) load only after you accept them in the cookie banner, only when we have switched them on, and the advertising pixels are switched off in our configuration today. When they run, they send data such as your IP address and browser details to those companies, which may build advertising audiences; for that they act as their own controllers under their own policies. The full list — every cookie and storage key, what it does, how long it stays, who sets it — is on the **Cookies** page, generated from the code. You can change your choice at any time through "Privacy choices".

## AI features

AI features are off until a company's administrator switches them on, and each person sees a notice and answers it before their first question. The question, the last few messages of the chat and the minimum context needed for the answer are sent to our AI model provider (listed on the Sub-processors page). Curahold keeps the question for as long as the company chose — not at all, 7, 30 (the default), 90 or 365 days — and does not keep the answer; an answer drawn only from the documentation may be reused for 7 days. Questions asked on our public website and documentation are not stored by Curahold. Nothing sent through these features is used to train any model.

**An AI assistant you connect yourself.** Users can also connect their own AI assistant (for example Claude or ChatGPT) to Curahold through our connector (MCP). It signs in as that user and sees only what the user can see; it makes changes (opens a task, logs a note, updates a task) only if the user allowed changes when connecting it. That assistant is not our sub-processor: what it reads goes to the AI company under the user's or the company's own agreement with it, and that agreement and its settings decide how long the AI company keeps the data and whether it uses it for training. The user can disconnect it at any time in their settings, and an administrator in the developer settings.

## Support session recording

Off by default. When a company turns it on, a support session can be recorded in the browser, with fields such as passwords masked; recordings stay in our own database for 7 days. Users are told when a session is being recorded [confirm in the product].

## Who receives it

Our service providers (sub-processors), each bound by a written contract — hosting and network, database, e-mail (Google Workspace, also for the product's own e-mails), payments (Paddle, our reseller), the AI model provider when AI is on, and Google or Microsoft only when a company turns on signing in with them. The current list with countries is on the **Sub-processors** page. Authorities, only when the law requires it.

## Transfers abroad

Some providers handle data outside your country — the countries are on the Sub-processors page. Where that happens we use the protections the law asks for: the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (with the UK addendum), the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified, the SDAIA clauses for Saudi Arabia, and, for Türkiye, the **standard contract under Article 9 of the KVKK** signed with each foreign recipient and notified to the Personal Data Protection Authority [status: being concluded — this sentence may only be published once the contracts are notified]. For Korea and Japan the recipients, countries and purposes are on the Sub-processors page and in the trial form's notice. **Saudi Arabia:** your data is currently stored outside the Kingdom; if a law in force comes to require storage inside the Kingdom we will tell you and offer a hosting option there.

## Your rights and how fast we answer

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its use (including at any time to direct marketing), and to withdraw any consent you gave — withdrawing is as easy as giving. If your account belongs to a company, its administrators can also export or erase your data. Write to [privacy e-mail]. We answer within **30 days** at the latest, and sooner where your law requires it: Egypt 6 working days, Korea 10 days, Taiwan 15 days for access, the EU/UK one month (extendable by two where the law allows). You can also complain to your data-protection authority (in Türkiye, the KVKK Authority).
