# Privacy policy

This policy explains how **[Company legal name]**, [address] ("Curahold", "we") handles personal data about the people who use Curahold and visit our websites. For the data our customers put into Curahold about their own customers and contacts, the customer decides what is processed and why; we process it on their behalf under the **Data processing agreement**. Turkish residents can also read our **KVKK notice**.

## What we collect

| What | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data | name, work email, company, role, language, time zone, profile picture | you, or the administrator who invites you |
| Sign-in and security data | sign-in times, device name, IP address, browser, the activity log | your use of the service |
| Usage data | which features are used, errors, performance | your use of the service |
| Billing data | the paying company, its billing contact, plan and invoices | Paddle, our reseller (we never see full card numbers) |
| Support data | help requests, screenshots you attach | you |
| Website data | pages visited, the language chosen | your browser |

## Why, and on what basis

- **To provide the service** you or your company signed up for: accounts, signing in, the features you use, notifications (contract).
- **To keep it secure**: protecting accounts, finding misuse, the device limit on seats (legitimate interest; legal obligation).
- **To support you** and tell you about the service: answering requests, important changes (contract; legitimate interest).
- **To improve the service**: understanding which features are used, in aggregate (legitimate interest).
- **Billing and accounting**, kept as the law requires (legal obligation).

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## Cookies and browser storage

Curahold sets only the cookies it needs to work: a session cookie (`app_session`) that keeps you signed in, and the language you chose. Browser storage keeps small conveniences on your device, such as the last board used or a filter. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

## Who receives it

Service providers who help run Curahold, under contracts that protect the data: Cloudflare (hosting and files), Supabase (database, EU region), Resend (email), Paddle (billing), and Google or Microsoft only when your company turns on signing in with them. The current list is on the **Sub-processors** page. Authorities, only when the law requires it.

## Transfers abroad

Some providers process data outside your country. Where that happens we use the protections the law asks for, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, and, for Türkiye, the standard contracts under Article 9 of the KVKK.

## How long we keep it

Account data while the account is open; after it closes, for [30] days so the company can export, then deleted. Security logs for [12] months. Billing records as long as tax law requires ([10] years in Türkiye). Backups are overwritten within [35] days.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw any consent you gave. If your account belongs to a company, its administrators can also export or erase a person's data from Settings. Write to [privacy email]; we answer within [30] days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority — KVKK).
