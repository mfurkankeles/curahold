# Quick add and shortcuts

Adding a task is as easy as writing one line. Press **N** (or **+ New → Task** on the left, the **+** at the bottom on a phone), write the task as it comes to mind, press **Enter**.

> Renewal call with Acme friday @Ayşe #Acme !high

That line becomes a task for Ayşe, on the customer Acme, due Friday, high priority. As you type, what was understood shows up under the line as small labels; the date label wears the colour of how soon it is.

## What you can write

| What | How | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Due date | Everyday words | today, tomorrow, friday, next week, weekend, end of month, nov 15, 15.11, in 3 days |
| Person | @ and a name | @Ayşe, @Ali Demir, @me |
| Customer | # and its name | #Acme, #Velto Mobility |
| Priority | ! and a level | !urgent, !high, !low |
| Repeat | every … | every day, every week, every monday, every 2 weeks, monthly |

Turkish works too: *yarın, cuma, cumaya kadar, haftaya, 15 kasım, her pazartesi, !yüksek, @ben*.

- Typing **@** or **#** opens a list; pick with the arrow keys and **Enter** or **Tab**.
- The **×** on a label: a person, customer or priority is taken out of the line. Words read as a date or a rhythm stay in the title (in quotes). If a word should never be read as a date, put it in quotes yourself: *"may" campaign report*.
- An @ or # that matches no one is shown with a yellow outline and stays in the title.

## Where you are matters

- Added from **My tasks**, the task is yours.
- On **a board**, it goes into the board's first column; change the board and column at the bottom of the window.
- On **a customer's page**, it is linked to that customer.
- On the **calendar**, the **+** on a day puts the task on that day.

## Several tasks at once

Paste a few lines (from meeting notes, say): each line becomes its own task, and all of them are shown before they are added. **Ctrl Enter** (**⌘ Enter** on a Mac) adds them all. With "Keep adding" ticked, the window stays open.

## Done in one click

In lists and on cards, each task has a round tick in front: click it and the task moves to the board's "done" column. A mistake? **Undo** in the notice that appears. The **Mark done** button in a task's window does the same.

## Keyboard shortcuts

They work everywhere except while you are typing in a box. **?** shows this list inside the app too.

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| N | New task |
| Ctrl K or / | Search and go |
| ? | Shortcuts |
| Esc | Close what is open |
| G then H | Home |
| G then M | My tasks |
| G then C | Calendar |
| G then A | Customers |
| G then P | Profile |
| G then S | Settings |

## On a phone

There are five buttons at the bottom: Home, My tasks, **+** in the middle, Customers and Calendar. Boards, settings and your menu are behind ☰ at the top left.

## Appearance

From your menu (your name at the bottom left) choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Same as this device**. The choice is kept on that device.
