# Sub-processors

> **Draft.** To be reviewed before it takes effect.

Last updated: [date]. New sub-processors are announced here and by email to administrators at least [30] days before they start.

| Company | What for | Where the data is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Hosting, the network, files | Global network; files in [R2 location] |
| Supabase, Inc. | Database | European Union ([region]) |
| Resend, Inc. | Sending email | [United States] |
| Paddle.com Market Ltd | Payments and invoices (merchant of record; billing contact of the paying company only) | United Kingdom and [ ] |
| Google LLC | Only when the company turns on "Sign in with Google" or connects Google Sheets | [ ] |
| Microsoft Corporation | Only when the company turns on "Sign in with Microsoft" or connects Excel | [ ] |
| Anthropic, PBC | The AI assistant (CuraAI, the docs' "Ask", the portal's questions box, first-reply drafts for support): the question, a few sections of our docs and only the workspace data the asking person may already see. Not used to train models. Only while the company keeps the assistant on (Settings → Support). | [United States] |

**Not our sub-processors:** the tools a company connects itself — its CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics 365, Pipedrive, Zoho, Bitrix24, Freshsales, Zendesk Sell), chat app (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat), support tool or AI assistant. Data moves to them on the company's instruction, under its own agreement with them.

**Session recording for support** adds no sub-processor: the recording library (rrweb) runs in the browser, and the recordings are kept in the same database as everything else (Supabase, above), for 7 days. A company can switch it off in Settings → Security.
