# KVKK notice (Türkiye)

> **Draft.** To be reviewed by a lawyer before it takes effect. The Turkish version is the binding one.

**[Company legal name]** ([address], MERSİS [number]), as data controller, informs you under Article 10 of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) as follows.

**Which data, and why.** Identity and contact data (name, work email, company, title), customer transaction data (plan, billing contact), security data (sign-in records, device, IP address, activity log) and data in support requests are processed to set up and run your account, keep it secure, provide support, invoice and meet legal obligations.

**Legal basis.** Processing necessary to set up or perform a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c), to meet a legal obligation (5/2-ç), and for our legitimate interests that do not harm your fundamental rights (5/2-f); collected electronically when you sign up and use the service.

**Who receives it.** Our service providers (hosting, database, email, payments — see *Sub-processors*), only as far as needed, and authorities when the law requires.

**Transfers abroad.** As some providers are abroad, data is transferred under Article 9 of the KVKK with appropriate safeguards (standard contracts notified to the Authority).

**Data our customers put in.** For the data a company records about its own customers and contacts in Curahold, that company is the data controller and Curahold processes it on its behalf (data processor).

**Your rights (Art. 11).** You may ask whether your data is processed and for information about it, its purpose and recipients; ask for correction, deletion or destruction; object to results arising only from automated processing; and claim compensation for unlawful processing. Apply in writing to [address] or to [KEP address / email] following the Communiqué on Application Procedures; we answer within 30 days at the latest.
