**Data our customers enter.** For the data a company keeps in Curahold about its own customers, contacts and staff, that company is the controller; Curahold processes it on the company's behalf as a **processor** (KVKK art. 12) under the Data processing agreement. Requests about that data go to the company.

**How we collect it.** Electronically, by automatic or partly automatic means, when you sign up, fill in the trial form, use the service, ask for support or visit the website. Non-essential cookies and advertising pixels run only with your explicit consent; details are on the **Cookies** page.

**Marketing.** The commercial-message approval (Law 6563) and the explicit consent to marketing processing are two separate, unticked, optional boxes and never a condition of the service. **Until Curahold is registered in İYS (the commercial-message system), these two boxes are not shown and we send no marketing e-mail to people in Türkiye.** Once it is, the approval is confirmed by e-mail the same day and registered in İYS within 3 business days; every message carries a refusal link, our trade name and MERSİS number, and a refusal takes effect within 3 business days at the latest.

**Transfers abroad (KVKK art. 9).** Some of our hosting, database, e-mail, payment and (when on) AI providers are abroad; the countries are on the **Sub-processors** page. As no adequacy decision exists for Türkiye, these transfers rest on **standard contracts** signed in Turkish with each foreign recipient and notified to the Authority within 5 business days of the last signature. **Your rights (KVKK art. 11).** To learn whether your data is processed; to ask for information about it; to learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; to ask for correction, and for deletion or destruction under art. 7 and for those third parties to be told; to object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

**How to apply.** In writing to [address], by KEP to [KEP address], or from the e-mail address registered with us to [privacy e-mail], as the Communiqué on applications requires. We answer within **30 days**, free of charge (if the action has a separate cost, the Board's tariff may apply). If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
