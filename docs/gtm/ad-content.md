# Curahold — Ad content drafts

Ready-to-adapt ad copy for paid acquisition, targeting the keyword **"task
management"** and the **CRM + task hybrid** angle. These are drafts for the owner
to review, edit and test — not a finished campaign.

**Honesty rules applied to every line below**

- No invented metrics (no "save 10 hours a week", no customer counts, no "#1").
- No testimonials, because there are no real ones yet.
- No "best / guaranteed / instant". Every claim maps to something the product
  actually does (tasks + CRM in one place, ~15-minute setup, free viewer seats,
  3-month free trial, cancel any month, English & Turkish, two-way CRM sync).
- Prices shown only where noted; Team is **$19/seat/mo** and Business
  **$35/seat/mo** (cheaper billed yearly), viewer seats free. Confirm prices on
  the landing page match before running any price-led ad.

---

## Core positioning

**One-liner (use everywhere):**
> **Your tasks and your customers, in one place.**

**Positioning statement (longer):**
> Curahold is a task manager and a lightweight CRM in one. Boards, tasks and
> renewals sit next to the customer accounts they belong to — alongside the chat
> app and CRM your team already uses, or on its own.

**Who it's for:** customer-success and account teams who currently run a to-do
app and a CRM side by side and copy work between them.

### Ad groups & keyword themes

Keep each ad group tight (one theme → its own pinned headline). Use these as
**themes**; the owner adds match types and a negative-keyword list.

**Ad group A — Task management (core keyword)**
- Theme/intent: people searching for a task or work management tool.
- Keyword themes: *task management software*, *task management tool*, *task
  manager for teams*, *team task tracking*, *work management app*.
- Pin headline: "Task Management, Done Right" or "Task Management + CRM".

**Ad group B — CRM + task hybrid (the differentiator)**
- Theme/intent: people who want tasks and customer data in one tool.
- Keyword themes: *CRM with task management*, *CRM and project management*, *task
  management with CRM*, *account management software*, *client management tool*.
- Pin headline: "CRM + Tasks in One Tool" or "Tasks and CRM, Together".

**Ad group C — Customer success / account teams (ICP)**
- Theme/intent: the target buyer by job.
- Keyword themes: *customer success software*, *renewal tracking tool*, *account
  management tool*, *client management software*, *CS workflow tool*.
- Pin headline: "One Tool for CS Teams" or "Boards, Tasks & Renewals".

> separate choice with trademark and cost implications — not included above.

## 2) Meta (Facebook / Instagram) — 3 ad concepts

Recommended lengths: primary text front-loads the first ~125 characters (shown
before "See more"); headline ≤ 40 characters; link description short.

### Concept A — "Two tabs become one" (pain: tool-switching)
- **Primary text:** Your to-do app says one thing. Your CRM says another.
  Curahold keeps tasks, boards and customer accounts in one place — so nothing
  gets copied twice or lost between tabs. Start free for 3 months.
- **Headline:** Tasks and CRM in one tool
- **Description:** Cancel any month
- **Visual concept:** Split frame. Left: two cluttered windows (a task list and a
  CRM) with copy-paste arrows between them. Right: a single clean Curahold board
  where a task card has an account + renewal chip attached. Brand navy
  (`#17133A`) field, teal (`#3FB596`) accent on the "one place" side.

### Concept B — "Built for CS & account teams" (ICP fit)
- **Primary text:** Renewals, follow-ups, and the accounts they belong to — in
  the same workspace. Built for customer-success and account teams who live
  between a task list and a CRM. Free read-only seats for the people who only
  need to look.
- **Headline:** Built for CS & account teams
- **Description:** Boards · accounts · renewals
- **Visual concept:** A board with columns "This week / Waiting / Done", each card
  carrying a small account chip; a banner shows an automation written as a
  sentence: *"When a renewal is 30 days away → open a task."* Light lavender
  (`#ECEAFE`) UI, navy text, teal buttons.

### Concept C — "Up and running in 15 minutes" (low friction)
- **Primary text:** Spin up your team's workspace in about 15 minutes, invite the
  team, and keep read-only viewers free. Simple monthly pricing and a 3-month
  free trial — cancel any month, no lock-in.
- **Headline:** Set up in 15 minutes
- **Description:** 3-month free trial
- **Visual concept:** A tidy onboarding screen with a progress bar, a subdomain
  field (`yourteam.curahold.com`) and teal checkmarks ticking down a short setup
  list. Calm, lots of whitespace.

## 3) TikTok — 3 short-video ad concepts

Native, captioned, vertical 9:16. Hook in the first ~2 seconds. No fake claims;
show the product doing real things. Scripts only — see `video-scripts.md` for the
two voiceover promos.

### Concept 1 — "The tab-switch loop" (~15s, relatable)
- **Hook (0–2s):** "POV: you're updating the same task in three different places."
- **Beats:**
  1. Fast flicks between a task app, a spreadsheet and a CRM; a tired sigh.
  2. Cut to Curahold: open one task card — the account and renewal are already on it.
  3. Beat of relief: "…oh."
- **On-screen text:** `task app → CRM → spreadsheet → 😵` then `one place → Curahold`
- **Caption/VO:** "Stop copying your work between tools. Tasks and customer
  records, together."

### Concept 2 — "CS team walkthrough" (~25s, demo)
- **Hook (0–2s):** "If you manage customer accounts, watch this."
- **Beats:**
  1. Screen-record a board; drag a card to "Done".
  2. Click an account; show its contacts and a renewal date.
  3. Build an automation by picking words in a sentence; toggle it on.
- **On-screen text:** `Boards + accounts + renewals` · `Automations in plain words`
- **Caption/VO:** "Your tasks, your accounts, and a nudge before a renewal slips
  — in one workspace."

### Concept 3 — "Set it up live" (~20s, speed)
- **Hook (0–2s):** "Let's set up a work tool in real time."
- **Beats:**
  1. Type a company name; pick a subdomain; choose a template.
  2. Invite a teammate; a starter board appears.
  3. Tap to add the first task.
- **On-screen text:** `~15 min, start to finish` · `3 months free`
- **Caption/VO:** "No long setup. Name your space, pick a template, invite the
  team. Three months free, cancel any month."

## 4) Landing message / value-prop block

Use on the campaign landing page (keep it consistent with the ads above).

**Hero headline:** One workspace for your tasks and your customers

**Subhead:** Most teams run a task app and a CRM side by side, copying between
them. Curahold puts boards, tasks, renewals and customer records in the same
place — next to the chat app and CRM you already use, or on its own.

**Three value bullets:**
- **Tasks + CRM together** — boards, subtasks, calendar and @mentions, right next
  to accounts, contacts and renewals.
- **Fits your stack** — two-way sync with your CRM, action buttons in your chat
  app, and the whole product in English and Turkish.
- **Easy to start** — set up in about 15 minutes, free read-only viewer seats, a
  3-month free trial, and monthly billing you can cancel any month.

**Primary CTA:** Start your 3-month free trial
**Secondary CTA:** See how it works

## 6) Decisions needed from the owner (ad / brand / targeting)

1. **Budget split across channels** — the model assumes Google 50% / Meta 30% /
   TikTok 20% as placeholders. Set the real split and the total monthly budget.
2. **Which channel(s) first** — recommend starting with Google Search (highest
   intent for "task management") before scaling Meta/TikTok; owner to confirm.
3. **Brand voice** — the drafts are plain and professional. Confirm tone (more
   playful? more enterprise?) before producing final creative.
4. **Price in ads or not** — decide whether to lead with price ($19/$35) or keep
   ads price-light and show pricing on the landing page.
5. **Competitor brand-term bidding** — yes/no on bidding against other tools'
   names (trademark + cost implications).
6. **Geo & language** — which countries, and the English/Turkish split per
   channel.
7. **Landing page** — confirm the destination URL and that its copy and prices
   match these ads before launch.
8. **Creative production** — who designs the Meta statics and films the TikToks
   (in-house vs agency), and whether to voice the promo scripts (see
   `video-scripts.md`; ElevenLabs is a paid step and a separate decision).
