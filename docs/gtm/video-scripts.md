# Curahold — Promo video scripts (voiceover)

Two short promo scripts — one **~30 seconds**, one **~60 seconds** — written for a
voiceover read. Each has scene beats, the voiceover (VO) line, and on-screen text.

> **Scripts only.** These are not produced videos. Turning them into voiced videos
> (for example with ElevenLabs text-to-speech) is a **paid step and a separate
> decision for the owner** — it is out of scope here. Nothing below has been sent
> to any paid service. See "Production notes & decision" at the end.

**Honesty:** no invented metrics, no testimonials, no superlatives. Every claim
reflects what the product does (tasks + CRM in one place, works with your chat app
and CRM or standalone, ~15-minute setup, free viewer seats, 3-month free trial,
cancel any month, English & Turkish).

**Brand:** navy `#17133A`, teal `#3FB596`, light lavender `#ECEAFE`. Logo is the
"C" whose open arc holds a dot.

## Script 1 — "In one place" (~30s)

*Pacing: brisk, ~80 words. Pair with clean UI screen-capture. Works as 16:9 and 9:16.*

| Time | On screen (visual) | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|------|--------------------|----------------|-----------|
| 0–5s | A cursor flicking between a task app, a spreadsheet and a CRM window | `to-dos here` · `customers there` | "You've got a task app for your to-dos, and a CRM for your customers — and you spend half your day copying between them." |
| 5–13s | The windows slide together into one Curahold board; a task card shows an account + renewal chip | `one workspace` | "Curahold puts them together. Boards, tasks and renewals, right next to the accounts they belong to." |
| 13–21s | Quick cuts: a message in a chat app with done/open buttons; a CRM-sync status tick; a language toggle EN/TR | `works with your stack` | "It works alongside the chat app and CRM you already use — or on its own. In English and Turkish." |
| 21–27s | A short setup bar filling; a subdomain `yourteam.curahold.com`; a "viewers free" chip | `~15 min · viewers free` | "Set up in about fifteen minutes. Free seats for the people who only need to look. Three months free to try." |
| 27–30s | Logo lockup on navy; teal dot settles into the "C" | **Curahold** · *Your tasks and your customers, in one place.* | "Curahold. Your tasks and your customers — in one place." |

## Script 2 — "A clearer week for your accounts" (~60s)

*Pacing: ~150 words, calmer read. Aimed at customer-success / account teams.*

| Time | On screen (visual) | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|------|--------------------|----------------|-----------|
| 0–6s | A person toggling between a to-do list and a CRM, re-typing the same note twice | `sound familiar?` | "If you look after customer accounts, your week probably lives in two tools: a task list, and a CRM. And a lot of copying in between." |
| 6–15s | Open Curahold; a board with columns "This week / Waiting / Done"; drag a card to Done | `one board for the week` | "Curahold brings them together. Plan your week on a board — drag a task to done when it's finished." |

| 37–46s | A chat-app message with done / tomorrow / open buttons; a CRM two-way-sync tick | `fits your chat app & CRM` | "Act on it from the chat app you already use, and keep it in sync with your CRM — both ways." |
| 46–55s | Setup bar; subdomain field; "viewers free" and "EN / TR" chips | `~15 min · free viewers · EN/TR` | "You can be set up in about fifteen minutes. Viewer seats are free, and it's in English and Turkish." |
| 55–60s | Logo lockup; pricing line small: *from $19/seat/mo*; "3 months free · cancel any month" | **Curahold** · *3 months free · cancel any month* | "Start free for three months, and cancel any month. Curahold — tasks and customers, together." |

## Production notes & decision

- **Format:** cut both as 16:9 (web/YouTube pre-roll) and 9:16 (TikTok, Reels,
  Shorts). Keep captions burned in — most views are muted.
- **Footage:** real product screen-capture only. Do not stage fake dashboards,
  fake numbers or fake customer logos.
- **Music:** light, modern, low-key; duck under the VO.
- **Voice:** a calm, clear, conversational read works for both scripts; pick one
  voice for consistency across the two. One English voice; a Turkish read of the
  same scripts can follow once the English is approved.
- **Decision needed from the owner (paid step):** whether to voice these with a
  text-to-speech service such as ElevenLabs (a paid subscription) or a human
  voice artist, and in which languages. No audio is generated until the owner
  chooses — these remain scripts only.
