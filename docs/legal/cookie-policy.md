This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies (local storage,
pixels, SDKs) used on our website and in the Curahold application, and how you
control them. It should be read with our **Privacy Policy**.

## 1. Consent first

We load **only strictly necessary** storage by default. Analytics and marketing
technologies load **only after you opt in** through our consent banner, and not
before. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via the **"Privacy
choices"** control shown on every page. Rejecting non-essential categories keeps
all of them switched off.

We group technologies into three categories:

### 1.1 Strictly necessary (always on)
Required to make the site and app work and to remember your consent choice.
These do not track you and cannot be switched off.

| Name | Purpose | Type | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `curahold_consent_v1` | Remembers your consent choice | Local storage (first-party) | Until cleared |
| `app_session` | Keeps you signed in to the application | Cookie (first-party) | Session / [30] days |
| `app_locale` | Remembers your language | Local storage (first-party) | Until cleared |

### 1.2 Analytics (consent required)
Help us understand how the product and site are used so we can improve them.

| Provider | Technology | Set only when | More info |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Analytics 4 | `_ga`, `_ga_*` cookies / gtag.js | You consent to **Analytics** | Google privacy terms |

We enable IP anonymisation and Google **Consent Mode** (analytics storage is
granted only on consent; advertising storage stays denied).

### 1.3 Marketing (consent required)
Let us measure campaigns and reach people who might find Curahold useful.

| Provider | Technology | Set only when | More info |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meta (Facebook) | Meta Pixel (`fbevents.js`, `_fbp`) | You consent to **Marketing** | Meta privacy terms |
| TikTok | TikTok Pixel (`events.js`) | You consent to **Marketing** | TikTok privacy terms |

All analytics and marketing identifiers above are configured from placeholders
the operator fills in; where an identifier is not configured, nothing from that
provider loads at all.

## 2. How to manage cookies

- **On our site/app:** use the **"Privacy choices"** control to accept, reject, or set each category, at any time.
- **In your browser:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Service from working.
- **Provider opt-outs:** the analytics and marketing providers above offer their own opt-out tools, described on their websites.

## 3. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Where required, we honour recognised browser privacy signals. Absent such a
signal, our default remains "nothing non-essential until you opt in".
