## 1. Definitions

- **Order** — the online sign-up, order form or written statement by which the Customer subscribes to a plan and a number of Seats.
- **Plan** — the subscription tier selected in the Order (e.g. Team, Business).
- **Seat** — one named user authorised to access the Service with write permissions; viewer users are provided at no charge.
- **Subscription Term** — the period described in Section 4.
- **Customer Data** — data the Customer or its users submit to the Service. Processing of personal data within Customer Data is governed by the **Data Processing Addendum (DPA)** and the **Privacy Policy**.
- **Fees** — the charges for the Plan and Seats as published at the Provider's current prices, exclusive of taxes.

## 2. The Service

2.1 The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Service during the Subscription Term for its internal business purposes, subject to this Agreement.

2.2 The Provider may improve, change or discontinue features, provided it does not materially reduce the core functionality of the Plan during a paid period.

2.3 **Acceptable use.** The Customer will not (a) resell or provide the Service to third parties except its own authorised users; (b) reverse engineer the Service except to the extent permitted by mandatory law; (c) upload unlawful content or use the Service to infringe the rights of others; or (d) attempt to breach the Service's security or multi-tenant isolation.

## 3. Free trial

3.1 **THREE-MONTH FREE TRIAL.** New customers may use the Service free of charge for an initial period of **three (3) months** from the start of the Subscription ("Trial Period"), with access to the features of the selected Plan, unless a different trial length is stated in the Order.

3.2 No Fees accrue during the Trial Period. No payment card is required to begin the Trial Period unless stated in the Order.

3.3 At the end of the Trial Period the Subscription continues as a paid Subscription under Section 4 **only if** the Customer has entered valid payment details or confirmed an Order; otherwise the Subscription will not convert to paid, and the account may be suspended and later deleted in line with Section 8 and the Privacy Policy retention terms. The Customer may cancel at any time during the Trial Period at no cost.

3.4 The Provider may limit trials to one per customer and may withdraw a trial in case of abuse.

## 4. Term, billing and the monthly exit right

4.1 **Rolling monthly term.** Unless an Order states a fixed term, the Subscription runs on a **month-to-month** basis. It begins on the Effective Date (or at the end of the Trial Period) and renews automatically for successive one-month periods until cancelled.

4.2 **MONTHLY EXIT RIGHT.** The Customer may cancel the Subscription **at any time**, effective at the end of the then-current monthly billing period. **The Customer pays only for the current month and leaves at month end** — there is no cancellation penalty and no obligation to pay for any period after the current month. Cancellation is made through the in-product billing settings or by written notice to [billing contact e-mail].

4.3 **No pro-rata refund for a cancelled month.** Fees already paid for the current month are not refunded on cancellation (the Service remains available until the end of that paid month), except where a refund is required by mandatory consumer or local law. Viewer seats remain free.

4.4 **Annual option.** If the Customer selects an annual Plan in the Order, Fees are payable in advance for the year; the monthly exit right in 4.2 does not apply to a committed annual term except as required by mandatory law, and the Service remains available until the end of the paid annual period if cancelled.

4.5 **Seats and changes.** The Customer may add Seats at any time; added Seats are billed on the next invoice and, for monthly terms, pro-rated for the current month. Reducing Seats takes effect at the start of the next monthly period.

4.6 **Payment.** Fees are charged through the Provider's payment processor/reseller named at checkout ([payment processor]). Taxes (VAT/KDV and any withholding) are added as required by law. Late or failed payment may lead to suspension after [number] days' notice.

4.7 **Price changes.** The Provider may change prices for future monthly periods on at least [30] days' notice; the Customer's continued use after the change takes effect is acceptance, and the Customer may exercise the monthly exit right if it does not agree.

After termination, the Provider will make Customer Data available for export for [30] days and then delete or anonymise it in line with the DPA and Privacy Policy, unless retention is required by law.
