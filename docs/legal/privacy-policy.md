## 2. What we collect

- **Account data:** name, business e-mail, organisation, role, language, and authentication metadata (device, session, last seen).
- **Billing data:** plan, seats, billing contact, and tax identifiers. Card details are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
- **Usage and diagnostics:** pages viewed, features used, error reports, and, only with your consent, analytics measurement (see the **Cookie Policy**).
- **Support data:** messages, attachments and, where enabled and consented, session recordings with inputs masked.
- **Website data:** limited server logs needed for security and to serve the site. Non-essential analytics and marketing identifiers load **only** after you opt in.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for automated decisions that

## 4. Consent, cookies and tracking

Non-essential cookies and tracking — including Google Analytics (GA4) and the
Meta and TikTok advertising pixels — load **only** after you give consent through
our consent banner, and never before. You can change or withdraw your choice at
any time using the "Privacy choices" control. Strictly necessary cookies (such
as remembering your consent choice and keeping you signed in) do not require
consent. See the **Cookie Policy** for the full list.

Subject to KVKK/GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, portability, and object to certain processing; and you may withdraw

notify affected parties and authorities of a personal-data breach as required by
KVKK and GDPR.
